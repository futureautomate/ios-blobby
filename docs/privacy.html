Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

Short version: Blobby does not collect, store or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What Blobby stores

Your habits, their history, your settings and the nickname and buddy you choose are saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive app data.

What Blobby does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, health or microphone access.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device by iOS. No notification content passes through any server.

Backups and sharing

If you use Export backup or share a progress card, Blobby creates a file or image and hands it to the place you choose (for example Files, iCloud Drive or a messaging app). What happens to it there is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

Purchases

If Blobby offers an in-app purchase, payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset all data in the app, or delete the app. Both remove everything Blobby stored on your device.

Children

Blobby does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
